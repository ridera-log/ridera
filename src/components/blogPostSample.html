Cost Factors

What Drives the Price

Not all Lagos moves are equal. Ikoyi to VI differs massively from Ikorodu to Lekki.

1

Distance & Location

The Farther, The Pricier

Lekki to Ajah is predictable. Ikeja to Ikoyi? Expect traffic cost. Mainland to Island adds toll gates and longer travel time. Every extra kilometre reflects on the final bill.

2

Floor Level

No Lift? Omo…

4th floor in Surulere with no elevator means higher labour cost. Staircase moves increase manpower, time, and physical effort. In Lagos, time is money.

3

Volume of Items

Bed & Fridge vs. Full House

Generator, deep freezer, washing machine, treadmill — the more you move, the bigger the truck required. That's why truck hire cost in Lagos differs from full-service moving cost.

4

Packing & Dismantling

Peace of Mind Costs Extra

Professional movers offer carton packing, bubble wrapping, TV padding, furniture dismantling & installation, and AC uninstallation. It adds to cost — but saves damage and chaos.

5

Estate Restrictions

Lekki, Ikoyi & VI Rules

Premium estates restrict truck entry times, charge access fees, and require damage deposits. This affects logistics planning and can inflate costs unexpectedly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked

Is moving more expensive during festive seasons?

Yes. December, end-of-month periods, and long weekends usually cost more due to higher demand.

Do movers in Lagos offer insurance?

Some do. Always confirm before booking — don't assume coverage is included.

How long does a move take?

1-Bedroom 2 – 3 hours
2-Bedroom 4 – 5 hours
3-Bedroom Full day (sometimes more)

Traffic can change everything.
